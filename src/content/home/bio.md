Associate professor at [LISTIC](https://www.univ-smb.fr/listic/) - [Université Savoie Mont Blanc](https://www.univ-smb.fr/).

I work on statistical signal and image processing, computational imaging.

**Current topics of interests are** : application of optimal transport in signal and image processing, scalable proximal optimization algorithms and applications in imaging, radio-interferometric imaging algorithms, remote sensing and covariance based machine learning algorithms

My interests lie at the frontier between mathematics and its application in constrained signal processing systems.

Teaching at the Electrical Engineering and Industrial Computing department (GEII) of [IUT d'Annecy](https://www.univ-smb.fr/iut-annecy/formation/genie-electrique/).
